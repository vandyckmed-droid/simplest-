How the score is built

12-1 is the log return from 12 months ago to 1 month ago; 6-1 runs from 6 months ago to the same point. Skipping the most recent month keeps short-term reversal out of a momentum signal.

Each return is annualised and divided by the annualised volatility of daily log returns over that same window, giving a unitless, Sharpe-like number. Blend is the average of the two. The drawer shows the annualised return, since an 11-month and a 5-month window are not otherwise comparable; the raw window return follows it.

Bars run left or right from the centre line, so the sign is readable without relying on colour. They are scaled to ; a handful of extreme scores peg the track.

Universe: the largest US-listed companies in each industry group, cut to roughly 25 apiece, each clearing $15M of median daily dollar volume over the last 63 sessions. Prices are split- and dividend-adjusted.

Momentum is a statistical tendency measured on past prices. Nothing here is a forecast or investment advice.
